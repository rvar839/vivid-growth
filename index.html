Help

How to read this

What each number means, and what the lever estimates assume.

New patient
A client whose first-ever appointment falls in the month.
New clients who joined
First-time clients who bought a membership or a package, divided by all first-time clients in the same period. The goal is —.
Rebook rate
Completed visits where the client booked another appointment, divided by all completed visits.
Active members
Membership starts minus cancels, counted at month end. The goal is —.
Green and red changes
Each change compares the latest month with the month before. Green means the number moved the right way.
Lever estimates
Each uses the last three months as its baseline. A new member is valued at the average monthly dues per member, a visit at the average spend per visit, and members added through the year are counted from the month they join. Package revenue from extra joiners, member spend above dues, and overlap between levers are left out, so treat the figures as a way to rank levers, not as a forecast.
Rebook timing
A visit counts as rebooked when the client's next appointment is booked by the end of the day after the visit.
The current month
The latest month is still in progress, so its totals grow until the month ends.
Joining window
A new client counts as having joined if they buy a membership or package within 30 days of their first visit.
Not in this app
Costs, profit and ad spend, because Mangomint does not send them.
