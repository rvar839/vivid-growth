Definitions

On track, At risk, Behind
Monthly totals are compared with the share of the month that has passed. Rates are compared with their goal. At risk means within 15% of where it should be.
New patient
A client whose first-ever appointment falls in the period.
Joined
A new client who buys a membership or package within 30 days of their first visit.
Rebook rate
Visits where the client's next appointment is booked by the end of the following day.
Active members
The starting count, plus memberships started, minus memberships cancelled.
Cancellations
Cancelled appointments as a share of all appointments in the month.
Yearly value of a lever
An estimate from the last three months, for ranking levers against each other. Not a forecast.
Not included
Costs, profit and ad spend. Mangomint does not send them.
